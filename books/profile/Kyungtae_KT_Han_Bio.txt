Professional Biography - Kyungtae (KT) Han

Kyungtae (KT) Han is a Senior Principal Researcher at Toyota Motor North America's InfoTech Labs, where he leads research on intelligent mobility systems, including generative AI agents, digital twins, personalized advanced driver-assistance systems, and V2X-enhanced cooperative driving. He has authored more than 90 peer-reviewed publications and is a named inventor on more than 100 U.S. patent filings. Before joining Toyota, he was a Senior Research Scientist at Intel Labs. He received his Ph.D. in electrical and computer engineering from The University of Texas at Austin. Dr. Han is an IEEE Senior Member, a recipient of SAE International's Vincent Bendix Automotive Electronics Engineering Award and the Intel Achievement Award, and the author of the forthcoming IEEE-Wiley textbook Generative AI for Connected and Autonomous Vehicles.
