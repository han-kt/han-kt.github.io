Research / Research area

Edge Computing and Resource-Aware AI

Research question

Which data should a connected system transmit, where should it compute, and what does AI execution cost? Collaborative research develops vehicular map and perception systems, information-centric networking, offloading methods, and measurements of on-device AI. These studies make latency, information freshness, and energy trade-offs explicit.

Methods & contributions

  1. Collaborative construction of live maps

    Live high-definition map construction divides work between vehicles and edge servers under transmission, computation, and latency constraints. COMAP studies proactive provisioning of crowdsourced maps.

    Zhang et al., IEEE TVT 2024 · COMAP, IEEE ICC 2023

  2. Selecting and compressing shared perception

    AdaMap combines object selection with adaptive point-cloud representation to share perception artifacts under latency constraints and changing network conditions.

    AdaMap, ACM/IEEE SEC 2023

  3. Named data, indexing, and information freshness

    DSORL selects map-data sources in vehicular named-data networking; edge-assisted indexing separates dynamic and static mixed-reality data; age-of-information-aware aggregation accounts for delayed and out-of-sequence updates.

    DSORL, IEEE T-ITS 2023 · Doe et al., Intell. Converged Netw. 2024 · Mallik et al., IEEE ICC 2024

  4. Computation beyond one vehicle

    CAVE studies passing vehicles as computation resources. Related work addresses reinforcement-learning offloading for mobile augmented reality and network-aware task distribution for edge-assisted generative AI.

    CAVE, IEEE GLOBECOM 2024 · REAL, IEEE TMC 2026 · Yan et al., IEEE ICC 2026

  5. Measuring AI before optimizing it

    lm-Meter profiles on-device language-model latency, while related studies measure and predict deep-learning energy across edge devices and examine how prompt properties affect on-device LLM energy.

    lm-Meter, ACM/IEEE SEC 2025 · Tu et al., ACM/IEEE SEC 2023 · Hu et al., EMNLP 2026

  6. Model and inference choices under resource constraints

    Energy predictors guide neural-architecture search in GreenAuto and PlatformX. Video-token sparsification reduces redundant visual input for driving question answering.

    GreenAuto, ACM HotMobile 2025 · PlatformX, ACM/IEEE SEC 2025 · Ma et al., IEEE IV 2025

Visual overview

Systems & evidence

  • Map and perception studies use simulated networking and emulation; the named-data-networking study uses a CARLA/ROS 2 environment. (Zhang et al., IEEE TVT 2024; AdaMap, ACM/IEEE SEC 2023; DSORL, IEEE T-ITS 2023)
  • Runtime profiling and energy studies measure AI execution on edge and mobile devices, complementing the networked-system evaluations. (lm-Meter, ACM/IEEE SEC 2025; Tu et al., ACM/IEEE SEC 2023; Hu et al., EMNLP 2026)
