Research / Continuing thread

Energy- and Resource-Efficient Computing

Research question

Earlier computing research addressed numerical precision, platform energy, interference measurement, and design-space exploration through specialized methods and experiments. This thread preserves those contributions alongside later measurements of energy and execution costs for on-device AI.

Contributions Evidence Publications

Contributions over time

  1. 2000–2009

    Fixed-point and wordlength optimization

    Sensitivity-guided wordlength search trades numerical output error against implementation complexity. Related work reduces data wordlength in DSP software and automates floating-point to fixed-point transformation. A later book collects this signal-processing work.

    Han and Evans, EURASIP JASP 2006 · Han et al., Asilomar 2006 · Han et al., IEEE SiPS 2004 · Han et al., IEEE ISCAS 2001 · Han and Evans, Book 2009

  2. 2008

    Identifying interconnect interference

    A bicoherence-based measurement study identifies high-speed digital interconnects in platform radio-frequency emissions.

    Han et al., ICCDCS 2008

  3. 2009–2013

    Low-power display subsystems

    Checksum-based image-change detection reduces display traffic for low-motion content. A separate hybrid DRAM and phase-change-memory frame buffer exploits read-dominant content; related work studies adaptive touch sampling.

    Han et al., IEEE ICCD 2009 · Han et al., ISLPED 2013 · Min et al., IEEE SysCon 2015

  4. 2015–2016

    Collaborative power and quality modeling

    Collaborative CAD work develops learning-based models of black-box hardware power, a polyhedral-based SystemC flow for low-power design-space exploration, and statistical models of approximate-hardware quality.

    Lee et al., IEEE/ACM ICCAD 2015 · Zuo et al., IEEE/ACM ICCAD 2015 · Lee et al., ISQED 2016

  5. 2023–2026

    Energy and latency of on-device AI

    Later work measures and predicts AI energy on edge devices, profiles on-device language-model latency, and uses energy predictors in neural-architecture search.

    Tu et al., ACM/IEEE SEC 2023 · lm-Meter, ACM/IEEE SEC 2025 · GreenAuto, ACM HotMobile 2025

Systems & evidence

  • Wordlength studies evaluate signal-processing designs; checksum-based display work includes an FPGA prototype; the hybrid frame buffer uses display-content and energy models. (Han and Evans, EURASIP JASP 2006; Han et al., IEEE ICCD 2009; Han et al., ISLPED 2013)
