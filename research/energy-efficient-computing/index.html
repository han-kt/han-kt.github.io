Research / Continuing thread

Energy- and Resource-Efficient Computing

A question that runs through this work is how to get acceptable accuracy, latency, and safety out of limited energy and compute budgets. It began with fixed-point and wordlength optimization for low-power signal processing, continued with display-subsystem power reduction and system-level power modeling, and now concerns the energy and latency of AI models on edge and in-vehicle hardware. In the connected-vehicle work between these periods, the same question appears as computation and bandwidth sharing between vehicles and edge servers (see Vehicular Edge Computing & Networking).

Themes over time

  1. 2000–2009

    Fixed-point and wordlength optimization

    Wordlength optimization and automatic floating-point to fixed-point transformation for low-power signal processing hardware and software, including sensitivity-guided wordlength search and data wordlength reduction for signal processing software, summarized in a 2009 book.

    Han and Evans, EURASIP JASP 2006 · Han et al., Asilomar 2006 · Han et al., IEEE SiPS 2004 · Han et al., IEEE ISCAS 2001 · Han and Evans, Book 2009

  2. 2009–2013

    Low-power display subsystems

    Energy reduction for display subsystems, including the use of checksums to cut display power for low-motion content and a hybrid DRAM and phase-change-memory frame buffer that exploits the read-dominant behavior of display content.

    Han et al., ISLPED 2013 · Han et al., IEEE ICCD 2009

  3. 2015

    System-level power modeling

    Learning-based power models of black-box hardware IPs, and a polyhedral-model SystemC generation flow for fast low-power design-space exploration (IEEE/ACM ICCAD 2015 Best Paper Award).

    Zuo et al., IEEE/ACM ICCAD 2015 · Lee et al., IEEE/ACM ICCAD 2015

  4. 2023–2026

    Energy and latency of on-device AI

    A runtime latency profiler for on-device language models; the effect of prompt variations on on-device LLM energy; a predictive energy model for mobile AI; measuring, predicting, and scoring the energy of deep learning across edge devices; and an automated platform that uses energy predictors to guide neural architecture search toward energy-efficient models for edge devices.

    lm-Meter, ACM/IEEE SEC 2025 · Hu et al., EMNLP 2026 · EPAM, IEEE ICC 2023 · Tu et al., ACM/IEEE SEC 2023 · GreenAuto, ACM HotMobile 2025

  5. 2026

    Deciding when to run costly reasoning

    Tonic meta-control keeps a persistent vigilance state that decides when deliberative reasoning modules run in a driving system, reducing compute relative to phasic-only triggering in a synthetic testbench and the highway-env simulator.

    Han et al., IEEE ITSC 2026
