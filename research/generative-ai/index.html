Research / Research area

Multimodal Scene Understanding & Language-Guided Driving

Language and vision-language models can describe driving scenes and respond to natural-language requests. The open questions are how well they understand multi-view sensor data, whether they can carry out a driver's instructions through existing driving functions, and whether they can run within in-vehicle compute and latency limits. This work builds benchmarks and datasets for driving-scene understanding, connects language models to driving functions and to dialogue with the driver, and studies efficient inference and language-aligned scenario generation.

Main themes

  1. Driving-scene understanding and its evaluation

    NuPlanQA, a multi-view question-answering benchmark and a dataset of one million question-answer pairs for driving scenes; PDB-Eval, a benchmark for describing and explaining personalized driving behavior from video; and concept-bottleneck models that explain driving control decisions with human-understandable concepts.

    NuPlanQA, ICCV 2025 · PDB-Eval, IEEE IV 2025 · Echterhoff et al., WACV 2024

  2. Language-guided driving

    LaMPilot, a framework and benchmark in which language models carry out user instructions such as "overtake the car ahead" by writing programs over established driving functions; and LLM4AD, a review and benchmark of language models for autonomous driving with experiments on vehicle platforms.

    LaMPilot, CVPR 2024 · LLM4AD, Proc. IEEE 2026

  3. Conversational driver assistance

    Scene-Aware Conversational ADAS (SC-ADAS) uses language models, vision-to-text scene interpretation, and structured function calling for multi-turn dialogue grounded in the driving scene, and executes ADAS actions only after the driver confirms them.

    Han et al., IEEE ITSC 2025

  4. Efficient multimodal inference

    Video token sparsification removes redundant visual tokens across consecutive frames, raising multimodal-LLM inference throughput by up to 33% and cutting memory use by up to 28% in driving visual question answering.

    Ma et al., IEEE IV 2025

  5. Driving-scenario generation and simulation

    SIMSplat, language-aligned 4D Gaussian splatting for generating driving scenarios; and NIVA, a probabilistic closed-loop multi-agent traffic simulator evaluated on the Waymo Open Motion Dataset.

    SIMSplat, ECCV 2026 · Lu et al., IEEE/RSJ IROS 2025

Systems and validation

  • Experiments on autonomous vehicle platforms comparing on-cloud and on-edge LLM deployment for personalized decision-making and motion control. (LLM4AD, Proc. IEEE 2026)
  • SC-ADAS implemented in the CARLA simulator with cloud-based generative models and evaluated on scene-aware and multi-turn interactions, including the latency added by vision-based context retrieval and the token growth from accumulated dialogue history. (Han et al., IEEE ITSC 2025)
