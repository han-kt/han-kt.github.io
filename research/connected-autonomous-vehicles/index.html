Research / Research area

Behavior Prediction and Human-Centered AI

Research question

How can an intelligent system anticipate nearby traffic while accounting for the person using it? Collaborative research spans surrounding-vehicle prediction, personalized car-following and trajectory models, driver monitoring, and trust-aware planning. Studies connect these models to assistance through naturalistic data, public benchmarks, human-in-the-loop simulation, and controlled vehicle experiments.

Methods & contributions

  1. Predicting surrounding-traffic acceleration

    Early surrounding-traffic studies use probabilistic LSTM models to predict acceleration and propagate vehicle state, and graph-convolution models to incorporate relationships among nearby vehicles.

    Jones and Han, IEEE IV 2019 · Su et al., IEEE ITSC 2020

  2. Learning personalized car-following methods

    Gaussian-process models learn individual car-following preferences; the 2022 journal study pairs learned preferences with a predictive safety filter. Inverse reinforcement learning learns preferences from historical driving, while later work adapts gap and acceleration settings from driver overrides.

    Wang et al., IEEE ITSC 2021 · Wang et al., IEEE T-ITS 2022 · Zhao et al., IEEE ICRA 2022 · Chen et al., IEEE ITSC 2025

  3. Personalized lane changes and interacting trajectories

    Driver digital twins support online personalized lane-change prediction. Related methods fuse vision and cloud information or use temporal graph networks to represent interactions in trajectory prediction.

    Liao et al., IEEE IoT-J 2023 · Liu et al., IEEE T-IV 2022 · Abdelraouf et al., IEEE ITSC 2023

  4. Sensing driver action, intention, and distraction

    M2DAR recognizes driver actions from multiple views; CEMFormer combines in-cabin and external video to predict intention; driver digital twins support online distraction recognition.

    M2DAR, CVPR Workshops 2023 · CEMFormer, IEEE ITSC 2023 · Ma et al., IEEE T-IV 2024

  5. Personalized decisions at yellow signals

    Dilemma-zone studies analyze individual stop-or-go behavior and evaluate a personalized transformer predictor in driving simulation.

    Qin et al., IEEE RA-L 2025 · Wei et al., IEEE FISTS 2024

  6. Human trust as a planning variable

    Human-subject studies examine changing trust in automated vehicles. Trust-aware route planning models trust as a partially observable human state and evaluates resulting routes in simulator studies.

    Sheng et al., IEEE ITSC 2019 · Sheng et al., ACM/IEEE ICCPS 2021 · Sheng et al., ACM TCPS 2022

Systems & evidence

  • Gaussian-process personalized ACC was evaluated numerically and in human-in-the-loop simulation. Later override-based gap and acceleration adaptation was tested in a real vehicle on a controlled test track. (Wang et al., IEEE T-ITS 2022; Zhao et al., IEEE ITSC 2023; Chen et al., IEEE ITSC 2025)
  • Online personalized lane-change prediction was evaluated in human-in-the-loop co-simulation and a connected passenger-vehicle field implementation. (Liao et al., IEEE IoT-J 2023)
  • Complementary advisory-speed research models driver speed-tracking error and includes simulator and passenger-vehicle evaluation. (Wang et al., IEEE T-IV 2020)
