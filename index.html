Senior Principal Researcher

Toyota Motor North America R&D, InfoTech Labs

Ph.D., Electrical and Computer Engineering, The University of Texas at Austin

I develop AI and computing methods for connected vehicles: digital twins for cooperative maneuvers, models of individual driving behavior, and language-guided assistance. I lead collaborative research that connects these questions to working systems, evaluated through driving data, simulation, computing testbeds, and controlled vehicle studies. Earlier contributions address energy-efficient computing and signal processing.

Selected work

Research Highlights

Digital twins for cooperative mobility

Collaborative work connects human, vehicle, and traffic digital twins to cloud–edge–device systems and cooperative merging.

Merging advisories were tested with passenger vehicles in a controlled field study; game-theoretic coordination was evaluated in Unity–SUMO co-simulation.

Liao et al., IEEE T-ITS 2022 · Liao et al., IEEE T-SMC 2022 · Wang et al., IEEE IoT-J 2022

Research details →

Learning how individual drivers behave

Collaborative methods personalize car-following and predict trajectories from driving history and vehicle interactions.

Naturalistic data and human-in-the-loop studies evaluate ACC and prediction; driver-digital-twin lane-change prediction also has a connected-vehicle field implementation.

Wang et al., IEEE ITSC 2021 · Wang et al., IEEE T-ITS 2022 · Zhao et al., IEEE ICRA 2022 · Abdelraouf et al., IEEE ITSC 2023 · Liao et al., IEEE IoT-J 2023

Research details →

Understanding scenes and programming assistance

Coauthored work provides NuPlanQA for multi-view scene understanding and LaMPilot for language-generated programs over established driving functions.

NuPlanQA benchmarks perception and spatial reasoning on driving imagery; LaMPilot evaluates instruction-following programs in simulation.

NuPlanQA, ICCV 2025 · LaMPilot, CVPR 2024

Research details →

Cooperative perception and measured edge AI

Collaborative work developed AdaMap’s adaptive point-cloud sharing and lm-Meter’s on-device language-model profiling.

AdaMap uses CARLA-trace emulation with simulated networking; lm-Meter measures latency on commercial mobile platforms. Related studies measure cross-device energy.

AdaMap, ACM/IEEE SEC 2023 · lm-Meter, ACM/IEEE SEC 2025 · Tu et al., ACM/IEEE SEC 2023

Research details →

Earlier foundations in efficient computing

Earlier contributions include sensitivity-guided fixed-point wordlength search, checksum-based image-change detection, and a hybrid display frame buffer.

Evaluations span wireless-demodulator designs, an FPGA image-change prototype, and display-content and energy models for the frame buffer.

Han and Evans, EURASIP JASP 2006 · Han et al., IEEE ICCD 2009 · Han et al., ISLPED 2013

Teaching

Teaching & Mentoring

Designing advanced research training in generative AI for vehicles and mentoring graduate researchers.

Internal research training Two-part curriculum · 2025 & 2026

Generative AI for Connected and Autonomous Vehicles

Designed and taught an advanced two-part curriculum at Toyota Motor North America's InfoTech Labs, combining technical foundations with hands-on applications to connected and autonomous vehicles.

Part I

2025

Language models, retrieval-augmented generation, function calling, fine-tuning, vision-language models, and agents for driving applications, with hands-on labs.

Part II

2026

Diffusion and flow-matching models, VAEs and GANs, video-language models, distillation for edge inference, closed-loop simulation, and evaluation, safety, and trust.

Graduate research mentoring

I mentor graduate research interns on collaborative studies, from formulating research questions to building prototypes and evaluating results.
