Kyungtae 'KT' Han

Senior Principal Researcher

Toyota Motor North America

Kyungtae (KT) Han is a Senior Principal Researcher at Toyota InfoTech Labs, where he leads pioneering work in AI-powered connected mobility, including Generative AI for Advanced Driver Assistance Systems, Automated Driving, and Vehicle-to-Everything safety technologies. He holds a Ph.D. from The University of Texas at Austin and an AI Graduate Certificate from Stanford University. With over 80 publications and more than 100 patents across communication systems, machine learning, and vehicular technologies, Dr. Han bridges academic excellence with industrial innovation. He has received the SAE Vincent Bendix Award, IEEE Best Application Award, and the Intel Achievement Award. A senior member of IEEE and an active member of ACM and SAE, he also serves as Associate Editor for the SAE Journal of Connected and Automated Vehicles. His research advances the future of intelligent, connected transportation.
