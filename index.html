I am a Senior Principal Researcher at Toyota Motor North America's InfoTech Labs, focusing on generative and agentic AI for connected and autonomous vehicles. My work spans multimodal driving intelligence, V2X-enabled cooperative autonomy, and AI-native mobility systems, with 90+ peer-reviewed publications and 100+ U.S. patents. Teaching

Teaching & Curriculum Development

Graduate-level course design and instructional development in AI for mobility systems.

Graduate-level Two-part series · Spring 2025 & Spring 2026

Generative AI for Connected and Autonomous Vehicles

An internal research training course at Toyota Motor North America's InfoTech Labs, moving from language-centric intelligence to full-stack generative autonomy for CAV systems. Curriculum directly informs the IEEE-Wiley textbook, Generative AI for Connected and Autonomous Vehicles (2026).

Part I · Spring 2025

15 weeks · rated 4.45/5

Focus: Large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, function calling, and agentic orchestration for CAV applications, with hands-on labs on prompt engineering, RAG, fine-tuning (LoRA, RLHF, GRPO), agent frameworks, vision-language models, and MCP / A2A protocols.

Part II · Spring 2026

11 sessions · completed August 2026

Focus: Generative model families beyond text, multimodal temporal reasoning, and safety-critical deployment — diffusion and flow matching, GANs and VAEs, VideoLLMs, knowledge distillation for edge inference, closed-loop simulation, evaluation of generative systems, and AI trust, alignment, and ethics.
