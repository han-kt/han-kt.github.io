Research in Generative AI for Connected and Autonomous Vehicles

I am a Senior Principal Researcher at Toyota Motor North America, leading research in generative and agentic AI for connected and autonomous vehicles. My work bridges deployable industry systems and scholarly research across multimodal driving intelligence, V2X-enabled autonomy, and real-time edge AI.

Email LinkedIn Google Scholar

Research Agenda

My research focuses on building AI systems that are both deployable in real-world mobility systems and grounded in scalable, rigorous learning frameworks.

Generative & Agentic AI for Driving Intelligence

Multimodal models and agent-based systems for scene understanding, conversational ADAS, and driving decision support.

Connected & Cooperative Autonomous Systems (V2X/CAV)

Communication-aware perception and planning for robust coordination across vehicles, infrastructure, and traffic networks.

Edge AI for Safety-Critical Mobility

Real-time inference, intelligent task distribution, and efficient deployment for latency- and reliability-constrained mobility systems.

Human-Centered ADAS and Personalization

Adaptive assistance models that account for driver behavior, trust, and user-specific interaction patterns.

Teaching & Curriculum Development

Graduate-level course design and instructional development in AI for mobility systems.

Generative AI for Connected and Autonomous Vehicles

An advanced course covering foundation models, multimodal reasoning, system integration, and deployment constraints in CAV systems.

  • Developed and taught as part of an advanced internal training program at Toyota Motor North America.
  • Course materials are actively evolving with current research and system case studies.
  • Directly connected to an upcoming IEEE-Wiley book.
  • Course Materials: Syllabus (2025) | Syllabus (2026, evolving)
  • Topics include: foundation models, multimodal reasoning, promptable ADAS, agent orchestration, safety and evaluation.
